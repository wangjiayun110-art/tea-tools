How to convert HEIC to JPG

1. Drop your .heic files in the box above, or click it to choose them. Several at once is fine.

2. Press Convert. The first HEIC loads a small decoder (about 1.3 MB); after that each photo takes a second or two. Everything happens in your browser.

3. Download the JPGs one by one, or all at once as a zip.

Stop the problem at the source

Converting fixes the photos you already have. To stop new ones being HEIC, change one setting on your iPhone: Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible. New photos will save as JPG from then on. Photos already on your phone stay HEIC — convert those here.

One more thing converting does for you: it strips the EXIF metadata, including the GPS location, so the JPG does not reveal where the photo was taken.

Questions people ask

What is a HEIC file?

HEIC is the photo format iPhones save by default since iOS 11. A HEIC photo is about half the size of the same photo as a JPG, but many Windows programs, Android apps and upload forms cannot open it. That is the whole problem: good photos, awkward format.

How do I stop my iPhone saving HEIC?

Go to Settings, then Camera, then Formats, and choose Most Compatible. New photos will save as JPG from then on. Photos already on your phone stay HEIC — convert those here.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser, on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, so private photos stay private.

Will the JPG be as good as the HEIC?

Almost. JPG is lossy, so a little detail is lost, but at 85% quality the difference is hard to see. The JPG will be larger than the HEIC — that is the price of a format everything can open.
